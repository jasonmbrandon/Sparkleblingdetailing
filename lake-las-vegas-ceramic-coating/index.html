Water spots are the enemy here

Around the lake, cars and trucks get hit with irrigation overspray and mineral-heavy water, then sit in the sun until the spots bake in. Trucks and SUVs that tow boats to Lake Mead pick up road tar and grime on the way. A ceramic coating makes the surface so slick that water beads and rolls off, carrying the minerals with it, and tar wipes away without scrubbing.

☀️ Heat & UV

Summers that regularly pass 110°F oxidize clear coat and fade reds and blacks. Coatings add a UV-resistant layer on top.

💧 Hard-water spots

Henderson water runs about 16 grains per gallon. On bare paint, spots etch in; on a coating they rinse away.

🌬️ Dust & swirls

Wiping dust off dry paint causes most swirl marks. A slick coating lets dust blow or rinse off instead.

Tow vehicles here usually get the 5-Year Graphene Armor plus the wheel-barrel coating to keep brake dust off.

Ceramic coating questions

Can you coat my truck or tow vehicle?

Yes. Full-size trucks and SUVs are priced by size; your quote covers the exact vehicle.

Do you coat boats?

Not at this time — we focus on cars, trucks and SUVs.

How much does ceramic coating cost?

Sedans start at $1,050 for our 3-Year Ceramic Shield, $1,575 for 5-Year Graphene and $2,625 for Lifetime. SUVs and trucks cost a little more. Paint correction is included in every package, and new clients get a $150 paint-prep credit.

How long does it take?

Plan on a full day for the 3-Year and 5-Year packages and up to two days for Lifetime. Most of that is paint correction — that is what makes the gloss.

How do I take care of it afterwards?

Keep it dry for the first 24 hours and skip soap for a week. After that, a gentle hand wash every couple of weeks is all it needs. We send you a simple care guide.
